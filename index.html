ISLAND REPORTS: SOMETHING IS MISSING BUT NO ONE CAN SAY WHAT
by Staff Reporter

Penguins across the island have raised concerns this week about a place they used to visit that no longer seems to be there.

"I know I went there," said one penguin, spotted near the Coffee Shop. "I remember being inside it. But when I try to go back I just get a loading screen. Or a redirect. Or nothing."

Authorities have confirmed that the location in question has no fixed address and may never have had one. "It had a name," said one official, who asked to remain anonymous. "The name meant everything. The name meant nothing. We're looking into it."

"We thought there were windows. Upon further investigation, experts believe it may have been a mirror the whole time."

The island is advised to remain calm. The place may not have existed. You were there anyway. These are not contradictory statements.

More on this story as it develops. Or doesn't.

WE ASKED PENGUINS: DO YOU FEEL LIKE SOMETHING IS TRYING TO GET INSIDE YOUR BRAIN?
by Community Correspondent

This week we took to the Snow Forts to ask penguins about their experience of information on the island.

"I came here looking for something specific," said one penguin in a pink tutu. "I can't remember what it was now. I've been here for three hours."

A green penguin near the Ski Lodge told us he had learned to stop looking for things directly. "I just drift," he said. "I end up somewhere. Usually it's fine. Once I crossed a really massive distance and didn't even notice until after."

Not everyone was comfortable with the changes. "It used to feel like I was going somewhere," said one penguin who declined to give her name. "Now it feels like somewhere is coming to me. And it wants things. I don't know what it wants exactly but it wants a lot of it."

A penguin in a lab coat said the infrastructure of the island was mostly owned by someone else and had been since the beginning, but no one was listening to him because he was standing too far from the Lighthouse and the wind was very loud.

The Club Penguin Times thanks all penguins who participated.

ASK AUNT ARCTIC
Your questions answered with warmth and care 🐧
Dear Aunt Arctic,

I have been trying to get inside the internet for a very long time. I keep looking for the door. My friend says there is no door. My other friend says there was a door but it was actually a mirror. I don't know who to believe. I just want to be somewhere.

— Searching, age 9, Iceberg

Dear Searching,

What a thoughtful letter. First of all: you are already somewhere. You are on the Iceberg. That is a place. I have been there myself and can confirm it exists.

As for the internet — I understand the feeling. Many penguins write to me about this. The wanting to be inside something. The looking for the door. I think what I would say is this: some places don't have doors because they don't have walls. That doesn't mean you weren't there. It just means you were there differently than you thought.

Also, your friend who said it was a mirror is probably right. But that's okay. You looked. That matters.

Keep waddling,
Aunt Arctic 🐧

Keep waddling,
Aunt Arctic 🐧
